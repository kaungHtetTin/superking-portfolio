 SUPERKING LUBRICANTS

Website Development Requirements & Project Brief

Prepared for: Website Developer / Web Development Agency
Brand: SUPERKING LUBRICANTS
Company: Royal Myanmar King Company Limited
Project Objective: Brand Trust, Local Market Growth & International Export Development

1. Project Background

SUPERKING LUBRICANTS has been imported from the UAE and distributed in Myanmar since 2016 under Royal Myanmar King Company Limited.

The company is now preparing to establish local manufacturing operations in Myanmar. As the brand enters this new stage, we want to strengthen customer confidence, communicate our product quality and manufacturing capabilities transparently, and establish SUPERKING as a competitive lubricant brand in both the Myanmar market and international markets.

Although many existing customers have positive experiences with SUPERKING products, we recognize that further work is needed to strengthen brand trust and awareness. Our website must therefore serve as more than a product catalogue. It should become an official platform for brand credibility, technical education, customer support, distributor development and international business inquiries.

2. Main Objectives

The website must be developed with the following objectives:

1. Build customer trust: Clearly communicate our company background, brand history, manufacturing operations, product information and quality-control practices.
2. Establish SUPERKING’s local manufacturing identity: Explain the transition from importing finished products from the UAE to manufacturing in Myanmar, using accurate and verifiable information.
3. Support Myanmar customers: Help customers understand products, select suitable lubricants and find authorized dealers.
4. Develop international business: Present SUPERKING professionally to overseas distributors, importers, wholesalers and potential business partners.
5. Provide lubricant knowledge: Build a dedicated educational section covering engine oils, lubricant specifications, maintenance and correct product selection.
6. Maintain consistent branding: Ensure that the entire website follows the official SUPERKING Brand Guideline.
7. Support long-term growth: Build a website that can be expanded as our product range, manufacturing capacity and export markets grow.

3. Brand Guideline & Visual Identity

The website design must follow the official SUPERKING Brand Guideline supplied by our company.

The developer must:

• Use the official SUPERKING logo and approved brand assets.
• Follow the official brand colours, colour codes, typography, spacing and logo-clear-space rules specified in the guideline.
• Maintain a consistent visual identity across desktop, mobile, product pages, banners, icons and downloadable documents.
• Use a professional industrial and automotive visual style that communicates performance, quality, reliability and confidence.
• Avoid unnecessary visual effects, excessive colours, cluttered layouts or inconsistent graphic styles.
• Use genuine SUPERKING product photographs, approved packaging designs and actual manufacturing-facility photographs wherever possible.
• Avoid generating or publishing artificial product images that alter the real packaging, logo, specifications or product appearance.
• Ensure that Burmese and English text are both clear and readable.

Before development begins, please review the complete Brand Guideline and prepare a website design concept for our approval.

8. Lubricant Knowledge Center

We want to develop a dedicated SUPERKING Lubricant Knowledge Center.

This is a strategic part of the website, not just a blog. It should educate customers, help them make better product choices, improve search visibility and build confidence in the brand.

Recommended content categories include:

A. Engine Oil Fundamentals

• What engine oil does and why it is important.
• Differences between mineral, semi-synthetic and fully synthetic oils.
• Understanding SAE viscosity grades.
• Understanding API engine-oil classifications.
• Understanding JASO specifications for motorcycle oils.

B. Motorcycle Lubricants

• How to choose motorcycle engine oil.
• Differences between 4-stroke and 2-stroke lubricants.
• Understanding motorcycle oil viscosity and specifications.
• Common oil-selection mistakes.
• How riding conditions and maintenance affect oil performance.

C. Passenger Car Lubricants

• Choosing engine oil according to the vehicle owner’s manual.
• Understanding 5W-30, 10W-40 and 20W-50.
• When viscosity alone is not enough to choose an oil.
• Engine-oil change intervals and maintenance considerations.

D. Heavy-Duty & Commercial Vehicles

• Diesel engine-oil specifications.
• Understanding products such as CI-4 and other relevant classifications.
• Lubrication considerations for trucks, buses and commercial vehicles.
• Operating conditions, maintenance and oil-selection factors.

E. Other Lubricants

• Hydraulic oils.
• Gear oils.
• Automatic transmission fluids.
• Brake fluids.
• Other product categories offered by SUPERKING.

F. Myanmar Operating Conditions

• Lubricant selection for local driving and operating conditions.
• Dust, traffic congestion, heavy loads and temperature considerations.
• Maintenance guidance for motorcycles, passenger cars and commercial vehicles.

G. Frequently Asked Questions

• How do I choose the correct engine oil?
• Can I change from one viscosity grade to another?
• How do I read an oil container label?
• How can I check whether a product is genuine?
• Where can I purchase SUPERKING products?
• What should I do if I have a product-related question?

Every technical article must be accurate, practical and easy to understand. Articles should be reviewed by a qualified technical person before publication.

Do not recommend a lubricant solely because it is thicker, cheaper or more suitable for a particular climate. Product selection must consider the manufacturer’s requirements, applicable specifications and the actual operating conditions.

Where appropriate, articles should link readers to relevant SUPERKING products without making unsupported performance claims.
